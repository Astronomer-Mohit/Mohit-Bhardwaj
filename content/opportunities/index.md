I welcome conversations with motivated students and researchers interested in radio astronomy, time-domain astrophysics, and the development of new observing capabilities. The exact availability of positions and projects depends on current funding, admissions cycles, and project needs; this page does not imply that a position is currently open.

## Research directions

Potential research topics in the group span several connected areas:

- **Fast radio bursts and radio transients:** source populations, burst environments, host galaxies, and the use of FRBs to study ionized matter across the Universe.
- **Radio interferometry and VLBI:** observing strategies, calibration, localization, and analysis of transient sources.
- **Radio instrumentation:** antenna and RF systems, signal processing, and digital backends for time-domain radio astronomy.
- **Computational and statistical astrophysics:** reproducible data analysis, population studies, and inference from astronomical surveys.

## Skills and preparation

Depending on the project, useful preparation may include programming (especially Python), data analysis, signal processing, electromagnetism, statistics, or astrophysics. Students from different academic backgrounds are welcome to explain their interests and relevant experience; the expected preparation varies by project.

I cannot guarantee a position or reply to every enquiry, but a clear note describing your interests and background helps me assess whether there may be a suitable research fit.
